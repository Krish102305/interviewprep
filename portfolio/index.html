Interview Connect

Practice like it's the real interview.

An interview-practice platform for college students and job seekers. AI builds a custom interview from the job description and your resume, an AI or human interviewer runs it live on camera, and AI grades every answer against the same rubric.

  • Hands-free AI interviewer. Speaks with natural ElevenLabs voices, listens when it stops talking, asks follow-ups grounded in what you just said.
  • Practice for a real job. Pick a real internship listing and get an interview built from its description.
  • Human interviews over WebRTC. Peer-to-peer video, live transcription, AI-generated interviewer guides, and fit-scored matching between students and interviewers.
  • Grading you can trust. Category scores, STAR analysis per answer, and quotes checked against the transcript so fabricated ones are dropped.
  • Fair conduct system. Warnings first, admin-reviewed reports, three strikes, and appeals. Tech problems never count as misconduct.
